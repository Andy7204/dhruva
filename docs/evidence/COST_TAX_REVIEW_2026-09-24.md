# Primary-source review for pending accounting repairs

Retrieved September24,2026. This is implementation research, not tax advice or a
completed repair. Source verification must accompany the eventual tests/config.

- https://groww.in/pricing : standard resident equity brokerage20 or0.1%, lower,
  floor5; equity delivery STT0.1% each side; NSE transaction0.00297%; SEBI0.0001%;
  IPFT0.0001%; delivery stamp0.015% buy. DP example20 before GST (depository3.5
  plus Groww16.5; a different depository rate is listed for women);18% GST applies
  to brokerage, DP, exchange, IPFT and SEBI components. Current config18.5 DP and
  omission of DP GST/IPFT require repair. Do not infer NRI pricing from snippets.
- https://groww.in/help/stocks%2C-f%26o%2C-ipo-%26-mtf/sx-pricing/what-is-stt :
  gold/liquid/gilt ETFs have no STT. Current equity-STT-on-all-assets model is wrong.
- https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/en/sale-of-shares and
  https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/capital-gain : distinguish listed equity,
  equity-oriented funds/business trusts and other instruments;20% specified STCG
  and12.5% specified LTCG above125,000 exemption, subject to conditions, cess and
  surcharge. Blanket allocation-basket tax classification is inappropriate.
- Section50AA source search identifies deemed-short-term treatment for specified
  mutual funds acquired since April2023. Direct source fetch failed; verify the
  current definition/acquisition-date applicability before implementing liquid,
  gold and silver ETF rules. Do not equate all non-equity instruments.

Required repairs include a shared account reserve, FIFO lots, separate tax basis
versus economic fee basis, explicit fiscal-year/loss treatment and honest omission
of investor-specific tax attributes. Existing research uses approximate constant
rates; historical metrics must retain that caveat and original data provenance.
