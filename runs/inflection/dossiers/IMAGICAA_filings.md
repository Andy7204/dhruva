Thanking you, Yours faithfully, For Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited Shweta Singh Company Secretary & Compliance Officer Membership Number: A44973 Encl: As above Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited Mumbai, 13th November 2026 – Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited (BSE: 539056; NSE: IMAGICAA), India’s largest amusement & Water Park player, today announced its unaudited financial results for the second quarter and half year ended September 30th 2026. H1FY27 Consolidated Performance Highlights:  Revenue from Operations grew 15% YoY to Rs. 218 Crs in H1 FY27 from Rs. 190 lakhs in H1 FY26, driven by robust footfall growth supported by an extended summer season and healthy demand across parks.  EBITDA increased 14% YoY to Rs. 72.3 Crs from 63.5 Crs in the corresponding period last year. EBITDA margin remained at 33% in H1 FY27. H1FY27 Consolidated Operational Highlights: Particulars H1FY27 H1FY26 Y-o-Y Growth Footfalls (in Lakhs) 13.65 11.71 16.5% ARPU (Rs) 1,420 1,377 3.2% Revenue (Crs) 217.99 189.99 14.7% Key Updates: • Novotel Divestment Status – o Board has approved the proposed divestment of its 287-key Novotel Imagicaa hotel at Khopoli to Juniper Hotels Limited for consideration of INR 248 crore o The divestment strengthens company’s balance sheet to sharpen its focus on the core entertainment business while unlocking capital for the next phase of growth • Bennett Coleman Deal – o Board has approved the issue of warrants for the issue of Equity shares worth Rs 16.25 Crs to Bennett Coleman & Co Ltd (BCCL) as part of a marketing deal. The funds would be utilized to strengthen brand visibility through advertising, marketing and promotional activities across the growing brand portfolio. • Acquisition of Shanku’s Water Park – o During the quarter, the company invested Rs. 50 Crs to acquire 50.002% share in Mehsana Next Parks Pvt. Ltd. which operates ‘Shanku’s Water Park’ in Mehsana, Gujarat. o IEL now manages the full operations of the park and have initiated expansion plans to make it an all-weather destination. Commenting on the Q2 & H1 FY27 performance, Jai Malpani, Managing Director, Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited said, "We continue to build on a strong foundation and a steadfast focus on delivering memorable experiences and enhancing guest satisfaction across locations. While Q2 is seasonally softer for the parks industry, our park revenues were up by 10% in Q2 FY27 despite 6% lower footfalls due to the high impact rains in the catchment in July-August. Our focus remains on strengthening operational performance and improving the overall guest experience through continuous enhancements across our offerings. We are seeing a good build up for Q3 FY27 already. We are also progressing with our expansion initiatives, with Hello Park Hyderabad expected to commence operations in the coming months. This will further strengthen our presence in the family entertainment segment and provide an additional avenue for growth. As we move ahead, we remain committed to enhancing guest engagement, strengthening our existing assets and building a scalable platform for sustainable long-term growth.” About Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited (BSE: 539056; NSE: IMAGICAA) owns and operates India's leading theme and water parks, including Imagicaa, WetnJoy , Sai Teerth, and Aqua Imagicaa at various locations. The company offers a wide range of exciting experiences, from thrilling rides and water slides to spiritual attractions, catering to families and tourists of all ages. Imagicaaworld is committed to providing world-class entertainment across its parks.
