Rationale The rating upgrade along with revision in the outlook of the long-term rating of E2E Networks Limited (E2E) favourably factors in the strong growth in revenues in FY2026 (Rs. 246 crore) and H1 FY2027 (Rs. 380 crore), with H1 FY2027 revenue representing a YoY growth of 375%, and ICRA’s expectations of sustenance of the same over the near to medium term. The rating is further supported by healthy utilisation and monetisation of its GPU infrastructure amid increasing demand for AI computing services, hence, aiding capacity expansion, and healthy customer additions. With continued healthy utilisation levels and ongoing deployment of additional GPU capacity, ICRA expects E2E to sustain the strong growth momentum over the near to medium term. E2E's operating margins have expanded steadily to 75% in H1 FY2027 from 51% in FY2026 and 44% in FY2022, supported by higher revenue realisations and improved utilisation levels. The rising scale of operations is expected to continue to support profitability over the medium term. Building on its industry experience and long -standing relationships with global technology partners, E2E has established a sizeable installed base of approximately 5,100 cloud GPUs as of September 2026 (3,900 in GPUs in FY2026) , positioning it among the leading domestic AI infrastructure providers. During H1 FY2027, the company secured a Rs. 1,000 -crore contract from a S overeign AI Company, valid until June 2029, which provides medium-term revenue visibility, and supports capacity utilisation. The revenue growth prospects are further supported by the company's participation in the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)'s IndiaAI Mission, aimed at expanding the country's AI computing infrastructure. Supported by these order wins, healthy capacity expansion plans and favourable demand prospects arising from the increasing adoption of AI across industries, the company is expected to maintain strong growth momentum over the medium term. Additionally, the rating continues to factor in the decade-long operational track record and extensive experience of its promoters in the cloud computing industry, specifically in delivering infrastructure -as-a service (IaaS) solutions to customers across diverse end-user industries. The rating is however limited by the capital-intensive nature of the business, which requires investments in GPU infrastructure to support growth and maintain technological competitiveness.
