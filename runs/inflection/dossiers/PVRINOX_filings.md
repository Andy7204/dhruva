Return on capital employed improved to 16.5% on a trailing twelve - month basis from 10.2% in FY'26, above the pre-COVID FY’20 level of around 13%. The Company generated INR 6,035 mn of H1 free cash flow and repaid INR 2,586 mn of debt. It completed its maiden INR 3,000 mn share buyback in September. Net cash increased to INR 1,407 mn at September-end, from INR 807 mn in June, even after the buyback. With PVR INOX turning net -cash positive, the Board today approved a Capital Allocation and Dividend Distribution Policy, broadening the earlier dividend -only policy. It prioritises growth investment in scree ns, premium formats and technology; net debt to LTM EBITDA within 0.5 times after any distribution; and returning around 30% of annual surplus cash flow through dividends and/or buybacks, at the Board's discretion. The policy is available on the Company's website. PVR INOX opened 24 screens across six cinemas in H1 FY'27, mainly asset -light, taking the network to 1,797 screens across 114 cities in India and Sri Lanka. It exited 25 screens, mostly loss- making. Of the 90-100 new screens planned for FY'27, most are expected in H2 under capital-light models. The first SMART Cinema opened in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, in September, offering an affordable capital-light format for Tier III and IV markets; two to three more are planned this fiscal. Looking ahead, the film slate spans languages. Hindi releases include Ramayana: Part One in November, Shah Rukh Khan's King in December, and Love & War and Golmaal 5 in Q4. Regional and pan-India titles include Chiranjeevi's Kaaka and Prabhas' Spirit. Hollywood's lineup includes The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, Avengers: Doomsday, Dune: Part Three and Jumanji: Open World, supporting a positive theatrical outlook. PVR INOX continues to drive footfalls and broaden engagement. Value offers include weekday morning shows at IN R 99, Friday first shows at INR 149 and front -row seats from INR 79. It is extending F&B through home delivery, Grab & Go counters, outdoor catering and its in -house food brand, while live events and sporting telecasts utilise screens outside peak film hours. Our margins are now well above pre -COVID levels, a clear sign of a structurally stronger business. We generated significant free cash flow, completed our maiden share buyback and carry a strong balance sheet. We remain firmly focused on growth, expanding our network through capital-light formats and taking cinema deeper into under -screened India with our new SMART Cinemas. With an exciting content pipeline, we are committed to delivering a compelling cinema experience and long-term shareholder value."
