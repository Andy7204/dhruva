Rationale and key rating drivers CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) has upgraded LT rating assigned to bank facilities of The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) to “CARE AAA; Stable ” from “CARE AA+; Stable ”, while reaffirmed the ST ratings at “CARE A1+”. The rating upgrade factors in continuous upliftment of its business and financial risk profile over the years as characterised by increase in revenue by over 3x in the last four years (FY22-FY26) with 16.3% y-o-y growth achieved in FY26, as IHCL continues to strengthen its market leadership position in the Indian hotels industry supported by its iconic Brand of “Taj” and pan-India network of hotels. Revenue growth has been supported by favourable demand conditions in the hospitality sector, sustained growth in travel and tourism, improving occupancy and room rates, healthy traction across business and leisure segments. Over the years, the company portfolio grew to 382 operational hotels with 33,609 room keys across brands with major being Taj, Vivanta, SeleQtions, Gateway, and Ginger, among others, as on June 30, 2026 (20,609 room keys as on March 31, 2022), while continuous focussing on asset light additions, which also supported its profitability margins . IHCL has 32%:68% mix of capital heavy to capital light (capital light includes hotels on operating leases and managed contracts) portfolio of operational hot els, which provides boost to its operating profitability considering higher contribution margin and is also expect ed to partially reduce volatility in operating profitability in downturn. The company’s profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation and taxation (PBILDT) margins remain strong at 33.02% in FY26 (against 31.08% in FY23). Strong operating profitability was supported by healthy revenue per available room (RevPAR) of ₹14,398 in FY26 increase from ₹13,428 in FY25. CareEdge Ratings expects the company’s operating profitability to remain healthy even in uncertain/ unfavourable industry situation and continues to remain higher than industry average owing to benefits arising from asset light expansion, continuous cost optimisation measures adoption and persistent of its strong brand value across segments. The company operates a diversified portfolio comprising luxury brands such as Taj, upscale brands such as SeleQtions and Vivanta, upper mid -scale offerings such as Gateway, and lean luxe/value brands such as Ginger, which provides resilience across demand cycles and customer segments. IHCL continues to maintain a robust financial risk profile, supported by a strong net worth, healthy cash flow generation, sizeable liquidity, and modest reliance on debt, with overall gearing improving to 0.22x as on March 31, 2026 (PY: 0.28x). Despite it s planned capex of ~₹3,600 crore in the medium term, CareEdge Ratings expects the company's leverage profile and debt coverage indicators to remain comfortable, supported by strong internal accruals and a net debt -negative position. IHCL's expansion strategy remains largely asset-light, with ~93% of additions through management contracts, thereby limiting capex requirements, project implementation risks, and balance sheet pressure while supporting sustainable growth. IHCL’s ratings continue to be underpinned by the strong support of its parent, Tata Sons Private Limited (TSPL, which holds a 35.66% stake). The company benefits from robust financial flexibility and high lender and investor confidence due to its Tata Group affiliation. There have been equity infusions in the past from the promoter group and CareEdge Ratings continues to factor the group support, as and when needed. Ratings continue to factor in IHCL’s susceptibility to economic slowdowns and seasonality in the hospitality sector, and presence of ongoing litigation with Mumbai Port Trust resulting in a significant contingent liability, though the same is pending for several years and in CareEdge Ratings view its impact is expected to remain moderate (if any against outcome), considering its strong liquidity position characterised by cash and cash equivalents of ₹4,325 crore as on March 31, 2026.
