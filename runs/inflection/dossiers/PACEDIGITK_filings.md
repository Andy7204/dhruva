This, along with the timely ramp-up and execution of the four BESS projects under the BOO model and the group's ability to scale up revenue while sustaining healthy profitability, will remain key monitorable. The order represents the largest BESS order secured by the Company to date. The scope of work includes BESS system supply, system integration, installation, testing, commissioning, civil works and 15-year operations & maintenance. The awarded scope encompasses Block-01 (300 MW / 900 MWh) and Block-02 (300 MW / 900 MWh) under Cluster-01 of the flagship 3300 MWh BESS Project at NTPC REL Khavda Solar Plant in Gujarat. The Company continues to strengthen its presence across India’s energy storage ecosystem through project execution for leading central nodal and state utility agencies. Following this landmark order win, the Company’s cumulative order inflows for FY2027 have reached Rs. 5,129 crore, elevating its total executable order book to Rs. 13,427 crore, supported by a consolidated BESS project portfolio of 6.62 GWh across EPC and BOO/BOOT models. Meeting the Class-I Local Supplier criteria (minimum 50% value of goods, services and works) was an important factor in securing this order . Pace Digitek Group’s investments in localisation, supported by 5 GWh of operational domestic BESS manufacturing capacity and its in-house container fabrication facility, strengthened its competitiveness in the bidding process. Commenting on the development, Mr. Venugopal rao Maddisetty, Chairman & Managing Director, Pace Digitek Limited, said: “Winning this landmark BESS order is a validation of Pace Digitek’s manufacturing scale, engineering depth and project execution capabilities. As a Class-I Local Supplier under domestic value addition guidelines, this award highlights the crucial role of local content and domestic manufacturing in advancing India’s energy storage goals. We have invested ahead of demand in expanding our manufacturing capacity, strengthening our project execution capabilities and developing greater manufacturing integration. With 5 GWh of capacity operational and our expansion towards 10 GWh progressing, we have built the manufacturing infrastructure to support growing market requirements.
