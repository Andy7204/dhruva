No. F9296 Encl.: As above A New Chapter in Food Pumping Technology Roto Pumps Launches ‘HYGENIX’ Series- Next Generation Hygienic Food Pumping Solutions at ANUGA Designed to address the evolving demands of food and Pharma processing Mumbai, India | 29, September 2026: Roto Pumps Ltd., a well-known global manufacturer of positive displacement pumping solutions, is showcasing and launching its next -generation Hygienic Food Pumping Solutions at ANUGA FoodTec Mumbai 2026 , taking place from 29 September to 1 October 2026 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre. At Hall 03, Booth J64, Roto Pumps is presenting its latest developments in hygienic pumping technology, designed to meet the demanding requirements of food and beverage processing applications, where product integrity, hygiene, reliability, and process efficiency are critical. Constructed from stainless steel smooth finish, the pumps are designed to support effective CIP (Cleaning-In-Place) processes. They are designed to meet the stringent requirements of 3-A and EHEDG standa rds (in process) , supporting hygienic processing, cleanability, and product integrity. The new portfolio includes Hygienic Progressive Cavity (PC) Pumps and Hygienic Twin Screw Pumps, expanding Roto Pumps’ capabilities for applications across the food, dairy, beverage, pharmaceutical and other hygiene-sensitive industries. Hygienic Progressive Cavity Pumps: Gentle, Controlled Product Handling Roto Pumps’ Hygienic Progressive Cavity Pumps are engineered for the controlled and reliable transfer of a wide range of food products and viscous media. From food mesh and dairy products to sauces, pastes and other processed food materials, the Roto Hygienic PC pump range is designed to provide dependable pumping performance while supporting stringent cleaning and sanitation standards. Hygienic Twin Screw Pumps: Flexibility Across Demanding Processes Twin screw technology provides flexibility for handling a broad range of products and process conditions. The pumps are designed to accommodate both low - and high -viscosity media while supporting gentle product handling and reliable process performance. This makes the technology suitable for a diverse range of applications across food, dairy, beverages, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics and other hygiene-sensitive processing environments. Designed Around Hygiene, Product Integrity and Process Reliability As food and Pharma manufacturers face increasingly stringent requirements around hygiene, product quality, cleaning and operational efficiency, pumping technology plays an important role in maintaining process performance. Roto Pumps’ new hygienic food pumping solutions have been developed with these requirements in mind, combining the company's expertise in positive displacement pumping with a focus on hygienic design and reliable fluid handling. The company's 3-A and EHEDG certifications for the hygienic pump range are currently under process, reinforcing Roto Pumps' commitment to meeting recognised hygienic design and food-processing requirements. About Roto Pumps Ltd. Established in 1968, Roto Pumps Ltd. is a global manufacturer of positive displacement pumping solutions, headquarters in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India specialising in Progressive Cavity Pumps and other engineered pumping technologies. With operations and customer s across multiple regions, the company serves diverse industries including wastewater, oil & gas, food & beverages, chemicals, paper & pulp, pharmaceuticals, mining, marine and other process industries. Roto Pumps continues to expand its technology portfol io to address evolving industry requirements and deliver reliable fluid-handling solutions for challenging applications.
