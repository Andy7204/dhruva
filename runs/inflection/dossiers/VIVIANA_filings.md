The facility has been established with a focus on manufacturing power transformers up to 20 MVA rating in the 66 kV and 33 kV classes, with an annual production capacity up to 1,000 MVA, and also provide facility to manufacture allied power equipment to cater to the growing requirements of the renewable energy, transmission and other power infrastructure sectors. The Company expects the facility to provide the following strategic benefits: - Backward and forward integration of the Company’s Power T&D EPC business; - Enhancement of in-house manufacturing capabilities for power transformers; - Ability to cater to the increasing demand for power equipment arising from power transmission, distribution and renewable energy segment; - Potential improvement in project execution capabilities and supply-chain efficiencies; - Broadening Company’s business portfolio in power equipment manufacturing segment and creation of an additional revenue stream; and; - Strengthening of the Company’s position as an integrated Power T&D infrastructure and equipment solutions provider.
