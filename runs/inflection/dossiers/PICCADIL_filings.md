The rating reflects the company's healthy business risk profile, driven by rapid scale-up of its premium alco-beverage business supported by its strong band presence, completion of capacity expansion, healthy profitability and comfortable financial risk profile. The rating also factors in the company's established position in the premium and luxury spirits segment, supported by strong acceptance of its flagship brand, Indri single malt whisky, and increasing geographical diversification through domestic and international markets. PAIL’s operating income grew at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 16% from fiscal 2020 to Rs 1,038 crore in fiscal 2026, driven by a 32% CAGR growth in revenue of distillery segment. PAIL is expected to witness healthy growth over the medium term, supported by favourable premiumisation trends in the alcoholic beverages industry and increasing scale of its branded portfolio. The company’s business risk profile has strengthened over the past few years, with the distillery segment’s share in total revenue increasing to 79% in fiscal 2026 from 48% in fiscal 2022, led by the strong performance of Indri and supported by other brands such as Camikara, Whistler, and Cashmir. Revenue growth is expected to remain at 50–60% in fiscal 2027 supported by increasing penetration of premium products, expansion into newer markets and ramp- up of recently-commissioned capacities. The growth in medium term is expected to be driven by ramp-up of significant capacity augmentation undertaken by the company over the past two years in distillery segment. It includes expansion of capacity at the Indri facility from 12 KLPD (Kilo Litres per day) to 30 KLPD for malt and from 78 KLPD to 220 KLPD for distillery operations, with commissioning of the greenfield capacity at Chhattisgarh 200 KLPD. Furthermore, sizeable ageing inventory and barrel stock accumulated by the company provide visibility on premium product launches and growth in the high-margin single malt category. PAIL’s operating margin has increased from 10% in fiscal 2020 to 22.5% in fiscal 2026 and is expected to be 22–24% in fiscal 2027 supported by increasing share of distillery segment. Financial risk profile is expected to remain strong with networth of Rs 901 crore as of March 2026 and the same is expected to increase beyond Rs 1,000 crore over the medium term. Healthy generation of accrual, with moderate dependence on external debt, has resulted in comfortable leverage metrics despite sizeable investments towards capacity expansion and inventory ageing. Gearing remained moderate at around 0.6 time as on March 31, 2026, and is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term, supported by healthy cash accrual and no plans of any major capital expenditure (capex). Liquidity remains strong, with cash and equivalents of Rs 70 crore as of June 2026. PAIL has availed a WCDL (working capital demand loan) of Rs 145 crore and its CC (cash credit) limits remain utilised at ~60% in fiscal 2026. Cash accrual is expected to remain sufficient to meet debt obligations, routine capex and incremental working capital requirement over the medium term.
